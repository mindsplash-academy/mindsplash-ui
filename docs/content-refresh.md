# Content refresh checklist

Review key pages at least once each quarter and whenever a programme, branch or curriculum changes. Assign a named academy reviewer before publishing factual changes.

Use [the organic search measurement guide](./seo-measurement.md) for monthly GSC/GA4 reporting and query-to-page tracking.

## Quarterly review

- Confirm programme names, curriculum routes, subjects and grade/year eligibility against current academy offerings and the student's school curriculum.
- Confirm branch names, addresses, phone details, operating hours and contact links against the current business records.
- Check that fee and timetable wording directs families to the academy for current details unless an approved current schedule is published.
- Review page titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, internal links and sitemap entries for pages added, changed or retired.
- Check visible headings, image alt text and that structured data matches content visitors can see.
- Review FAQ answers, forms and calls to action for accuracy and usability.
- Review expert profiles and article attribution; add credential or publication details only when the academy has supplied and approved them.

## Annual results and programme review

- Reconfirm programme descriptions and subject availability for the new academic year.
- Add student scores, ranks, awards, case studies or testimonials only after the academy verifies the source evidence and obtains permission to publish identifiable student information.
- Label each result with its cohort or exam year, the assessment and the verified score/rank. Keep historical results clearly marked as historical; remove or archive claims that cannot be substantiated.
- Do not imply that an individual result is typical or guaranteed.

## Before publishing

- Have an academy subject-matter reviewer approve factual claims.
- Make sure every new page has a unique title and description, one clear H1, useful links to related programme/subject/location/contact pages, and a canonical URL.
- Check that FAQ schema and other structured data match the visible page exactly.
- Record the reviewer and review date in the content change or release notes.
